A peering is two fragments on two Vpc objects other releases own — one entry in each network's
spec.vpcPeerings and one static route in each network's spec.staticRoutes. The platform's reconciler
writes them through the co-owned apply (docs/plan/09 § A second writer on an object); Helm cannot.
templates/fragments.yaml shows both shapes, and values.yaml is the surface they are rendered from.
